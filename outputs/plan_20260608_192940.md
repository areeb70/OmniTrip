### Transit Recommendations

**Tokyo** boasts one of the most efficient public transportation systems in the world.

*   **Recommended:** Utilizing the extensive **trains and subway networks** is highly recommended for efficient travel across the city. Walking is also an excellent way to experience many of **Tokyo**'s districts.
*   **Backup Options:** Rideshare apps (e.g., Uber) are available but generally more expensive than public transport. Taxis are also readily available.
*   **OmniTrip Tip:** Consider purchasing a local city-pass (e.g., Suica or Pasmo IC card) for unlimited or easy tap-and-go travel on public transport, significantly enhancing convenience.

### Safety & Risk Assessment

**Tokyo** is renowned for its exceptional safety.

*   **Safety Level:** Very Low Risk
*   **Key Considerations:**
    *   Always check current travel advisories from your government before departure.
    *   Keep both physical and digital copies of your passport and important documents separate.
    *   For public Wi-Fi, using a secure VPN is advisable to protect sensitive information.
    *   Familiarize yourself with local emergency numbers (e.g., 110 for police, 119 for fire/ambulance).

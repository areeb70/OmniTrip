### Detailed Itinerary: Paris for Two

This itinerary balances structured exploration with ample time for spontaneous romantic moments.

**Day 1: Arrival & Eiffel Charm**
*   **Morning:** Depart from **New York** (JFK/EWR) to **Paris** (CDG). Upon arrival, transfer to your charming Parisian hotel. Check-in and refresh.
*   **Afternoon:** Begin your Parisian adventure with a visit to the iconic **Eiffel Tower**. Ascend to the summit for breathtaking panoramic views of the city. Afterwards, enjoy a leisurely stroll through the **Champ de Mars** and **Trocadéro Gardens**.
*   **Evening:** Indulge in a romantic dinner at a classic French bistro with views of the illuminated Eiffel Tower. Consider a enchanting **Seine River cruise** as the city lights up.

**Day 2: Art, History & Riverside Romance**
*   **Morning:** Immerse yourselves in art and history at the world-renowned **Louvre Museum**. Focus on key masterpieces like the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo.
*   **Afternoon:** Enjoy a romantic walk through the serene **Tuileries Garden**, leading to **Place de la Concorde**. Cross the **Pont des Arts** (Lover's Bridge) and enjoy the riverside atmosphere.
*   **Evening:** Explore the vibrant **Le Marais** district, known for its historic architecture, trendy boutiques, and cozy cafes. Enjoy a gourmet dinner in this charming neighborhood, perhaps followed by a classic French cabaret show.

**Day 3: Bohemian Rhapsody & Grand Avenues**
*   **Morning:** Discover the bohemian charm of **Montmartre**. Visit the majestic **Sacré-Cœur Basilica** for stunning city views and wander through the artistic **Place du Tertre**.
*   **Afternoon:** Experience the grandeur of **Paris** with a walk along the famous **Champs-Élysées**, leading to the magnificent **Arc de Triomphe**. Ascend the Arc for another spectacular city vista.
*   **Evening:** Savor a final romantic dinner experience, perhaps at a Michelin-starred restaurant or a cozy wine bar, enjoying exquisite French cuisine and wine.

**Day 4: Parisian Farewell & Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely breakfast. Explore **Île de la Cité** to admire the exterior of **Notre Dame Cathedral** and the beautiful **Sainte-Chapelle**. Take a final stroll through the intellectual **Saint-Germain-des-Prés** quarter for some last-minute souvenir shopping.
*   **Afternoon:** Enjoy a farewell lunch. Depending on your flight schedule, proceed to **Paris** (CDG) airport for your departure back to **New York**.

### Safety & Travel Considerations

Our `Risk Data` indicates a **Low safety level** for **Paris**. However, as with any major city, it's wise to remain vigilant. Key considerations include:
*   Checking current travel advisories from your government.
*   Keeping a digital copy of your passport and other important documents.
*   Using a secure VPN for public Wi-Fi networks to protect personal data.
*   Familiarizing yourselves with local emergency numbers (e.g., 112 for all emergencies in France).
*   Being aware of pickpockets, especially in crowded tourist areas.

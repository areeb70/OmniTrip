## 3. Proposed Itinerary: Romantic Delhi Rendezvous

This itinerary blends historical grandeur with romantic moments, ensuring a seamless and enriching experience.

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Imperial Grandeur**
    *   **Morning/Afternoon:** Arrive at **Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)**. A pre-arranged private transfer will take you to your luxury accommodation. Check-in and take time to relax and acclimate.
    *   **Late Afternoon:** Begin your exploration with a visit to the iconic **India Gate**, a war memorial, followed by a leisurely stroll along the tree-lined **Rajpath**.
    *   **Evening:** Enjoy a romantic welcome dinner at a high-end restaurant in **Connaught Place**, offering a blend of international and fine Indian cuisine, or a charming rooftop dining experience.

*   **Day 2: UNESCO Wonders & Serene Gardens**
    *   **Morning:** Discover the architectural magnificence of **Humayun's Tomb**, a stunning 16th-century Mughal masterpiece and a UNESCO World Heritage site, often considered a precursor to the Taj Mahal – perfect for tranquil couple's photos.
    *   **Afternoon:** Explore the towering **Qutub Minar Complex**, another UNESCO site showcasing early Indo-Islamic architecture. Afterwards, find serenity amidst the blooming beauty of the **Garden of Five Senses**, designed to engage all senses and offering a truly romantic ambiance.
    *   **Evening:** Indulge in a gourmet dinner experience at one of Delhi's celebrated fine dining establishments, perhaps exploring the vibrant culinary scene of Khan Market.

*   **Day 3: Old Delhi Charm & Mughal Majesty**
    *   **Morning:** Witness the historical grandeur of the **Red Fort** (exterior viewing recommended, interior can be crowded) and visit the magnificent **Jama Masjid**, one of India's largest mosques.
    *   **Afternoon:** Immerse yourselves in the bustling, historic lanes of **Chandni Chowk**. Enjoy a guided rickshaw ride, sampling local delicacies and street food from reputable vendors, and explore the diverse markets for unique souvenirs.
    *   **Evening:** A relaxing evening awaits. Consider a rejuvenating couple's spa treatment at your hotel or a luxurious dinner offering exquisite regional Indian cuisine.

*   **Day 4: Leisure & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely breakfast at your hotel. Depending on your flight schedule, you might have time for some last-minute luxury shopping at a high-end mall or a peaceful walk through **Lodi Garden**, known for its ancient tombs and lush greenery.
    *   **Afternoon:** A private transfer will take you back to **Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)** for your onward journey, carrying cherished memories of your romantic Delhi escapade.

## 4. Transportation Guide

For a comfortable and reliable experience in **New Delhi**, we recommend the following:

*   **Recommended:** Utilizing private car services with a dedicated driver for daily excursions, especially for couples seeking comfort and efficiency. This allows for seamless travel between sites without the hassle of navigating local public transport.
*   **Backup:** Reputable rideshare apps such as Uber and Ola are widely available and offer convenient, metered services. Ensure you have a local SIM card or international roaming for easy booking.
*   **OmniTrip Tip:** While not for all journeys, consider an auto-rickshaw for short, local experiences or a metro ride during off-peak hours to truly experience the city's pulse. For longer stays, a local city-pass might offer value, but for 4 days, private hire is more suitable for luxury travel.

## 5. Safety & Risk Mitigation

New Delhi is a vibrant city, and while generally safe for tourists, a medium safety level indicates the need for awareness and precautions.

*   **General Considerations:**
    *   **Travel Advisories:** Always check the latest travel advisories issued by your government before and during your trip.
    *   **Documentation:** Keep digital copies of your passport, visa, and other important documents on your phone and a cloud service, separate from physical copies.
    *   **Connectivity:** Use a secure VPN when connecting to public Wi-Fi networks to protect your data.
    *   **Emergency Contacts:** Research local emergency numbers (Police, Ambulance) and have them readily accessible. Your hotel concierge can also be a valuable resource.
    *   **Health:** Consult your doctor about recommended vaccinations and carry any necessary personal medications. Drink only bottled water.

*   **Risk Mitigation & Backup Plans:**
    *   **Minor Theft:** Be vigilant in crowded areas like markets; keep valuables secure and out of sight. Consider using a travel-safe belt or a crossbody bag.
    *   **Transportation Safety:** When using taxis or rideshares, confirm the driver's identity and the vehicle details before boarding. Share your trip details with a trusted contact. Opt for pre-booked hotel transfers for airport pick-ups/drop-offs.
    *   **Health Emergencies:** Ensure you have comprehensive travel insurance that covers medical emergencies and evacuation. Keep a list of local hospitals and clinics.
    *   **Cultural Sensitivity:** Dress modestly, especially when visiting religious sites, and be aware of local customs to avoid misunderstandings.

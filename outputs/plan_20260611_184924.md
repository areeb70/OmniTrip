## 1. Introduction

OmniTrip is delighted to present this bespoke travel dossier for your exquisite journey to **Srinagar**, the summer capital of Jammu and Kashmir, India. Known for its serene lakes, majestic Mughal gardens, and vibrant houseboats, Srinagar promises a captivating blend of natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. This itinerary is designed to offer a comfortable yet immersive experience, leveraging current market pricing to ensure a seamless and memorable trip.

## 2. Travel Overview

Your journey will commence from **New Delhi**, flying directly into the heart of Kashmir. The plan focuses on experiencing Srinagar's iconic attractions, with a day trip to the picturesque meadows of **Gulmarg**, ensuring a diverse and enriching adventure.

## 3. Detailed Itinerary

This 4-day itinerary has been crafted to maximize your experience in and around Srinagar, balancing sightseeing with leisure.

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Dal Lake Serenity**
    *   **Morning:** Arrive at **Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport (Srinagar)**. You will be greeted by your private transfer and taken to your hotel for check-in.
    *   **Afternoon:** Enjoy a relaxing Shikara ride on the iconic **Dal Lake**, exploring its floating gardens, market, and charming houseboats.
    *   **Evening:** Stroll along the Boulevard Road overlooking the lake, followed by dinner at a local restaurant savoring authentic Kashmiri Wazwan cuisine.

*   **Day 2: Mughal Marvels & Old City Charm**
    *   **Morning:** Embark on a tour of Srinagar's magnificent **Mughal Gardens**: **Nishat Bagh (Garden of Joy)**, **Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love)**, and **Chashma Shahi (Royal Spring)**, each offering unique horticultural beauty and architectural grandeur.
    *   **Afternoon:** Explore the historic **Old City of Srinagar**, visiting the revered **Jamia Masjid** and the sacred **Hazratbal Shrine**. Discover local handicraft markets.
    *   **Evening:** Enjoy a delightful dinner experience, perhaps at a renowned local eatery.

*   **Day 3: Gulmarg Alpine Adventure**
    *   **Morning:** Take a scenic drive to **Gulmarg**, known as the "Meadow of Flowers," a renowned hill station and skiing destination.
    *   **Afternoon:** Experience the famous **Gulmarg Gondola**, one of the highest cable cars in the world. Ascend to Apharwat Peak for breathtaking panoramic views (Phase 1 & Phase 2). Enjoy leisurely walks amidst the alpine scenery.
    *   **Evening:** Return to Srinagar, followed by a relaxed evening and dinner.

*   **Day 4: Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely breakfast. Depending on your flight schedule, you may have time for some last-minute souvenir shopping for Pashmina shawls, Kashmiri handicrafts, or dried fruits.
    *   **Afternoon:** Private transfer to Sheikh ul-Alam International Airport (Srinagar) for your flight back to **New Delhi**.

## II. Luxury Itinerary: Paris - A Romantic Journey

This curated itinerary balances iconic Parisian experiences with opportunities for intimate moments and sophisticated leisure.

**Day 1: Arrival & Seine Splendor**
*   **Morning:** Depart from New York (JFK/EWR) to **Paris** (CDG). Upon arrival, a private chauffeur will greet you for a comfortable transfer to your luxurious hotel in the elegant **Saint-Germain-des-Prés** or historic **Le Marais** district. Settle in and unwind.
*   **Afternoon:** Enjoy a gentle introductory stroll through your charming neighborhood, perhaps a visit to the exterior of the magnificent **Notre Dame Cathedral** and the quaint streets of **Île de la Cité**. Indulge in a classic Parisian café experience.
*   **Evening:** A romantic welcome dinner awaits at a pre-booked Michelin-starred restaurant, followed by an exclusive **Seine River dinner cruise**, offering breathtaking views of illuminated monuments such as the **Louvre**, **Musée d'Orsay**, and the sparkling **Eiffel Tower**.

**Day 2: Artistic Grandeur & Eiffel Tower Romance**
*   **Morning:** Embark on a private, guided tour of the world-renowned **Louvre Museum**, allowing you to explore its masterpieces with expert insights and avoid the crowds.
*   **Afternoon:** Leisurely wander through the beautifully manicured **Tuileries Garden**, leading to a sophisticated shopping experience along **Rue Saint-Honoré** or a leisurely walk up the grand **Champs-Élysées** to the majestic **Arc de Triomphe**.
*   **Evening:** Savor a gourmet dinner at an acclaimed restaurant with stunning views of the **Eiffel Tower**. Conclude your day with an ascent to the summit of the **Eiffel Tower** for an unforgettable champagne toast as the city lights twinkle below.

**Day 3: Royal Extravaganza & Bohemian Charm**
*   **Morning:** A private chauffeur will transport you to the opulent **Palace of Versailles**. Enjoy a pre-arranged, private guided tour of the Royal Apartments, the awe-inspiring Hall of Mirrors, and a leisurely exploration of the magnificent gardens.
*   **Afternoon:** Return to **Paris** and immerse yourselves in the bohemian charm of **Montmartre**. Visit the iconic **Sacré-Cœur Basilica**, explore the artistic hub of Place du Tertre, and perhaps commission a portrait from a local artist.
*   **Evening:** Indulge in a romantic dinner at a charming bistro in Montmartre, or experience the dazzling spectacle of a classic Parisian cabaret show like the **Moulin Rouge** (optional, requires advance booking).

**Day 4: Parisian Farewell & Departure**
*   **Morning:** Enjoy a leisurely and decadent Parisian breakfast at your hotel or a renowned patisserie. Take some time for last-minute souvenir shopping or a final romantic stroll.
*   **Late Morning/Lunch:** A relaxed brunch at a charming local eatery.
*   **Afternoon:** Your private chauffeur will provide a seamless transfer back to **Paris** (CDG) for your departure flight to New York.

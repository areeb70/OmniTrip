OmniTrip is delighted to present a meticulously crafted dossier for your romantic 4-day escape to **Paris**, the City of Love. This itinerary is designed for a couple departing from **New York**, focusing on intimate experiences, iconic landmarks, and the unparalleled charm of the French capital. Prepare for an unforgettable journey filled with romance, culture, and culinary delights.

## Proposed Itinerary: 3 Nights / 4 Days in **Paris**

This itinerary blends iconic sightseeing with opportunities for romantic moments, ensuring a memorable experience for two.

*   **Day 1: Arrival & Enchanting Evenings**
    *   **Morning:** Arrive at Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) Airport, transfer to your charming boutique hotel. Settle in and enjoy a light Parisian brunch.
    *   **Afternoon:** Begin your Parisian adventure with a leisurely stroll through the **Tuileries Garden**, leading to the iconic **Musée du Louvre** (exterior appreciation or select gallery visit).
    *   **Evening:** Experience the magic of **Paris** with a romantic **Seine River Cruise**, offering breathtaking views of illuminated landmarks. Conclude with a gourmet dinner in the vibrant Saint-Germain-des-Prés district.

*   **Day 2: Imperial Grandeur & Bohemian Charm**
    *   **Morning:** Ascend to the **Arc de Triomphe** for panoramic views of the city. Afterwards, enjoy a leisurely walk down the famous **Champs-Élysées**, perhaps stopping for a coffee.
    *   **Afternoon:** Explore the historic **Opéra Garnier**, a masterpiece of Baroque architecture, or delve into the art treasures of the **Musée d'Orsay** (known for Impressionist works).
    *   **Evening:** Discover the artistic haven of **Montmartre**. Visit the stunning **Sacre-Cœur Basilica** and wander through the charming Place du Tertre. Enjoy a cozy dinner in a traditional Montmartre bistro.

*   **Day 3: Iconic Views & Parisian Romance**
    *   **Morning:** A quintessential **Paris** experience: visit the **Eiffel Tower**. Consider an early ascent for fewer crowds and spectacular morning views.
    *   **Afternoon:** Explore the charming streets of Le Marais district, known for its historic architecture, elegant boutiques, and delightful cafes. Enjoy a macaron tasting at a renowned patisserie.
    *   **Evening:** Indulge in a romantic dinner at a restaurant with an Eiffel Tower view or experience a classic cabaret show, such as the Moulin Rouge, for a truly Parisian night out.

*   **Day 4: Farewell & Departure**
    *   **Morning:** Enjoy a final Parisian breakfast. Depending on your flight schedule, explore a local market, pick up some last-minute souvenirs, or revisit a favorite spot.
    *   **Afternoon:** Transfer to Paris Charles de Gaulle (CDG) Airport for your return journey to **New York**, carrying cherished memories of your romantic escape.

## Travel & Transit in **Paris**

Navigating **Paris** is part of its charm. OmniTrip recommends:

*   **Recommended Transit:** The **Paris** Metro system is highly efficient, extensive, and the best way to get around the city. Walking is also highly encouraged to soak in the Parisian atmosphere.
*   **Backup Options:** Rideshare applications such as Uber are readily available for convenience, especially late at night or for specific destinations.
*   **OmniTrip Tip:** Consider purchasing a Navigo Découverte pass (if staying for a full week, otherwise individual tickets or a Carnet of 10 tickets) or a Paris Visite travel pass for unlimited travel on public transport, which can be cost-effective and convenient.

## Safety & Considerations

Your safety and peace of mind are paramount. **Paris** generally has a low safety risk for tourists.

*   **General Safety:** Exercise standard urban precautions. Be aware of your surroundings, especially in crowded tourist areas and on public transport.
*   **Key Considerations:**
    *   Always check current travel advisories from your government prior to departure.
    *   Keep digital copies of essential documents, such as your passport and visa (if applicable), separate from physical copies.
    *   Use a secure VPN if connecting to public Wi-Fi networks.
    *   Familiarize yourself with local emergency numbers (e.g., 112 for all emergencies in Europe).
